# Determine the current (2026) capital cost per kW of small modular reactors across leading designs and regions, to support technology comparison and investment/deployment decisions against large nuclear and other generation options.

**Research date:** 2026-09-13 · **Sources analyzed:** 12 · **Evidence records:** 50 · **Verified claims:** 50

---

## Executive Summary

Current SMR capital costs are $11,600–20,000/kW for first-of-a-kind projects, 4–7x above vendor NOAK targets of $3,000–8,000/kW. The strongest evidence is realized outcomes: NuScale's cancelled CFPP ended at ~$20,000/kW after 74% escalation, and Natrium sits at ~$11,600/kW — at or above Vogtle's $12,000/kW large-reactor outcome and far above Korea's ~$2,500/kW. The biggest uncertainty is whether learning curves deliver NOAK targets; bottom-up versus top-down estimates diverge 2–3x. Recommendation: base investment decisions on $11,600–20,000/kW FOAK costs, treat vendor targets as unvalidated aspirations, and note SMRs currently exceed renewable OCCs ($1,484–3,711/kW) and most large-nuclear benchmarks.

## Scenario Model: SMR capital cost per kW (USD/kW, real 2026, total project cost basis; Darlington BWRX-300 FOAK as central case)
