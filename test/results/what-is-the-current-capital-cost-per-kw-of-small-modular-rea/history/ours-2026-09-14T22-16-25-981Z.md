# Determine the current (2026) capital cost per kW of small modular reactors across leading designs and geographies, with credible ranges and drivers, to support investment, procurement, or policy decisions on SMR deployment.

**Research date:** 2026-09-14 · **Sources analyzed:** 15 · **Evidence records:** 64 · **Verified claims:** 60

---

## Executive Summary

Credible 2026 SMR capital costs cluster at $14,000–$20,000/kW, not the $2,250–$3,720/kW vendor targets. Independent/utility estimates anchor the range: MIT's FOAK BWRX-300 at $14,000/kW, TVA at $17,949/kW, OPG Darlington at ~$17,400/kW, NuScale's cancelled project at $20,139/kW, and TerraPower at $28,986/kW. These exclude financing and owner's costs, which add materially. The widest uncertainty is FOAK-to-NOAK learning and a -30%/+50% estimate accuracy. Recommendation: base procurement and policy on independent bottom-up estimates, treat vendor figures as optimistic targets, and budget for financing costs, schedule slippage, and supply-chain escalation.

## Financing Sensitivity, Cost Drivers, and Deployment Status

Financing costs materially shape SMR capital economics. In MIT's scenario mirroring OPG's Darlington plan, financing eight additional BWRX-300 units averages roughly $19 billion in total financed capital, versus $20–30 billion initial cost for two AP1000s [1]. Interest rates are a key driver: the NuScale/UAMPS cost model's rate rose about 200 basis points since July 2020, helping push construction cost up 75% ($5.3B to $9.3B, 2021–2023) [2]. These figures carry high source confidence (0.90–0.92).

Deployment milestones determine when cost certainty arrives. Rolls-Royce SMR's UK GDA Step 3 began July 2024, after Step 2 (April 2023–July 2024) (inference — no direct source)[10]; GDA should conclude in 2026, with final investment decision not before 2029 and first power mid-2030s [8]. On April 15, 2026, Rolls-Royce and Great British Energy-Nuclear contracted site-specific design for three 470 MWe units at Wylfa, backed by up to £599 million from the National Wealth Fund and £2.6 billion in the 2025 Spending Review [8]. In Canada, Ontario approved OPG's $7.7-billion spend ($6.1B first BWRX-300 plus $1.6B shared infrastructure) in May 2025 — the first G7 SMR [9] — though completion slipped a year from 2028 after a later-than-expected CNSC construction licence [9].
