# Determine realistic current capital cost ranges (USD/kW) for small modular reactors to inform investment, procurement, or policy decisions on SMR deployment.

**Research date:** 2026-09-15 · **Sources analyzed:** 16 · **Evidence records:** 93 · **Verified claims:** 88

---

## Executive Summary

Realistic SMR capital costs exceed most vendor claims. Independent overnight estimates cluster at roughly $7,300–$8,900/kW (2023$), well above vendor-reported $2,850–$5,100/kWe that exclude financing; the UAMPS project reached $20,139/kWe once financing and escalation are included. Strongest evidence: the systematic three-tier pattern — vendor, independent, and project-inclusive. Biggest uncertainty: the FOAK-to-NOAK gap — a 1.10 FOAK factor is implausibly small against a ~7x realized spread, and no credible NOAK cost evidence yet exists. Recommendation: treat vendor overnight figures as optimistic lower bounds, and anchor investment, procurement, and policy decisions on finance-inclusive, escalated benchmarks until multiple NOAK units deliver real cost data.

---

## Realistic current capital cost ranges (USD/kW) by leading SMR design

Decision-grade data are uneven. Only NuScale has a clean, published overnight capital cost (OCC): VOYGR-12 at $2,850/kWe (2018 USD; 12 modules, 924 MWe; excluding financing) [1]. The same review's most recent NuScale figure — $20,139/kWe (2022 USD; 6 modules, 462 MWe) — includes financing, so the ~$2,850–$20,139/kWe spread reflects scope, vintage, and financing treatment, not a single design cost [1][1]. NuScale's own current statement, $4,200/kW (earlier $5,000/kW), states no dollar year or basis [2].

Independent non-vendor baselines cluster far higher: S&L 2020 at $7,288/kW (2023 USD) [5]; S&L 2023 at $8,936/kW (2023 USD) [5]; EIA ATB Moderate at $8,942/kW (2023 USD) [6]; EIA AEO2023 at $8,349/kW (2022 USD) [6]. Confidence is high for the INL-cited and EIA figures; the GEH and Rolls-Royce values are design targets or derived figures that should not be treated as decision-grade current costs.
