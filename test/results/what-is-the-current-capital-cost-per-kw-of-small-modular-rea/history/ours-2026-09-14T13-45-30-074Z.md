# Determine the current capital cost per kW (overnight cost, USD/kW) of leading small modular reactor designs — including the realistic range from vendor estimates to actual project disclosures, and FOAK vs NOAK trajectories — to support an SMR procurement or investment decision.

**Research date:** 2026-09-14 · **Sources analyzed:** 12 · **Evidence records:** 75 · **Verified claims:** 74

---

## Executive Summary

Realistic SMR capital costs span roughly $2,850–20,139/kW, with the spread driven less by design than by cost basis: financing inclusion, scope, and year-of-dollar. NOAK-oriented estimates cluster at $3,500–5,000/kW, but FOAK project disclosures consistently exceed vendor targets by 40–75%+: UAMPS reached $20,139/kW (financing-inclusive), Darlington ~C$17,400/kW, while derived BWRX-300 Poland figures sit near $4,840/kW. Strongest evidence: INL modeling shows FOAK-to-NOAK multi-module learning cutting costs ~50% ($8,282 to $4,173/kWe). Biggest uncertainty: modeled estimates carry -30%/+50% accuracy, and no consistent cost basis exists across sources. Recommendation: budget FOAK procurement on financing-inclusive project disclosures, not vendor targets, and structure purchases around multi-module co-siting to capture learning.
