# Determine the realistic current per-kW capital cost range for SMRs and the credibility of vendor and institutional estimates to guide an investment or power-procurement decision

**Research date:** 2026-09-13 · **Sources analyzed:** 12 · **Evidence records:** 43 · **Verified claims:** 43

---

## Executive Summary

Realistic SMR capital costs depend entirely on FOAK vs NOAK status. Vendor NOAK figures ($2,250–3,720/kW) are credible only for mature multi-unit fleets; independent FOAK estimates run $14,000–20,139/kW including financing (MIT BWRX-300 $14,000/kW; IEEFA UAMPS $20,139/kWe), with INL re-normalization placing most advanced-reactor estimates at $2,000–8,000/kWe. The FOAK-to-NOAK gap (roughly 2–7x) is the strongest evidence and the biggest driver of decision risk. Major uncertainties remain unverified: LCOE decomposition, component cost breakdown, HALEU supply chain, and IRA/DOE policy offsets. Recommendation: budget at the independent FOAK range, treat vendor NOAK claims as fleet-scale targets, and price financing and schedule risk explicitly.
