## Market Size, Composition, and Growth Trajectory

The global private credit market reached US$3.5 trillion in AUM as of December 2025, per the ACC/AIMA "Financing the Economy 2025" report published with Houlihan Lokey [1]. Deployment grew to US$592.8bn in 2024, up 78% from US$333.4bn in 2023 [2]. Europe accounts for close to 30% of global AUM [1], with activity diversified across corporate, asset-backed, real estate, and infrastructure lending [1].

| Segment | Size / trajectory |
|---|---|
| Subscription lines | ~US$1tn total; US$190bn Fitch-rated (~19% of that market) [s6] |
| NAV-based facilities | ~US$100bn today; ~US$600bn by 2030 (FFA estimate) [s3][s8] |
| US middle-market/BDC maturities | ~US$475bn through 2033; ~US$260bn due 2026–2029 — a ramp, not a cliff [s12] |

Middle-market direct loans priced at SOFR+480–500bps, with all-in yields above 9% for many first-lien packages [s12]. The evidence base covers 49 managers with US$2.1tn AUM, including Apollo, Ares, Blackstone Credit, Blue Owl, and Carlyle [1]; investors are 76% institutional and 24% retail/mass-affluent [1]. Confidence is high for AUM and deployment figures, though the US$3.5tn figure rests on a single source and the NAV projection is an estimate, not observed data.

## Scenario Model: Private credit weighted-average default rate (broadest definition, % of principal) — the pivotal uncertainty is default/non-accrual escalation under a 2008-style rate shock

**Base estimate:** 2.67% of principal (Proskauer Q4 2024 Private Credit Default Index, broadest definition incl. covenant defaults; 825 US senior-secured/unitranche loans, $152.8bn original principal). Narrower comparison bases: 1.8% ACC/AIMA corporate non-accrual (2025), 1.45% BDC nonaccrual at cost (Q4 2025), 1.2% payment-style default rate (Proskauer).

| Scenario | Assumption | 2027 (12m) | 2028 (24m) | 2029 (36m) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative — 2008-style rate shock (escalation to observed stress levels) | A 2008-style shock spikes SOFR back above the prior-year 4.33% print and widens spreads; defaults escalate to the highest evidence-stated stress anchors — the Proskauer $25-49.9M EBITDA band (4.7%) and the Fitch broadly-syndicated TTM rate (5.2%) — as the $260bn 2026-2029 maturity ramp reprices into the heaviest 2028-2031 stacks. | 4.7% (Proskauer $25-49.9M EBITDA stress band) | 5.2% (Fitch BSL TTM level, Nov 2024) | 5.2% (persists through heaviest maturity stacks) |
| Base — range-bound, no shock | Rates hold near the April 2026 SOFR of 3.65% (below the 4.33% prior-year level); defaults stay within the observed 1.95-2.71% band (Proskauer Q2-Q4 2024) as refinancings (28% of 2025 direct lending, +29% YoY) absorb the maturity ramp rather than forcing distressed exits. | 2.7% | 2.7% | 2.7% |
